Pongsakorn (Vita) Sukonthamarn is a BioSciences PhD student specialising in human immunogenetics, with a thesis focus on understanding how genetic variation shapes immune responses. My work explores the diversity and function of immune cell receptors in underrepresented populations, with a particular focus on T cell receptors (TCRs) and Human Leukocyte Antigens (HLA), and investigates how host genetic backgrounds influence adaptive immunity.

He also experiences in wetlab approaches, using iPSC-derived macrophages, to investigate how ancestral genetic variation regulates innate immune responses. Beyond human immunogenetics, he is broadly interested in comparative immunogenetics and how immune systems have evolved across diverse animal species.
