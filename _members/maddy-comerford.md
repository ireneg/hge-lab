I am a postdoctoral researcher interested in the consequences of archaic introgression and, more broadly, understanding human evolutionary history. My work in the Human Genomics & Evolution group @ SVI focuses on characterising archaic introgression in Island Southeast Asia, with a particular focus on Denisovan introgression.

I have expertise in both wet-lab and dry-lab techniques, with extensive experience in functional genomics methods including massively parallel reporter assays. More recently, I am focusing on emerging bioinformatic methods for variant effect prediction, with the aim of applying these tools to understand Denisovan introgression.

I have taught data analysis classes in the School of Mathematics and Statistics @ the University of Melbourne since 2023 and am very interested in principles of good data visualisation.
